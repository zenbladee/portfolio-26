Here are a few of the software projects I've worked on!

Anonymous Tips Page

A simple web page for the UCSD community to submit their tips anonymously. There are multiple options for students and staff to submit tips including a google form as our least encrypted option, an email with a public key for our most encrypted option, and additional instructions for those who would like to submit tips in-person or directly to certain staff members. This page relies on SNO.

Split-LAMP Primer Design Tool

A web tool that automates the design of Split-LAMP primers for bioinformatics applications. The tool is built using Python with Flask for the back-end and HTML, CSS, and JavaScript for the front-end. It utilizes bioinformatics libraries to analyze sequences and generate optimal primers.
